Three directions for the Top Cleaning homepage

Top Cleaning already has its content: four services, a four-step process, and a phone number that does most of the selling. What it does not have is a form — a way of arranging that content so a stranger in Chișinău trusts it in five seconds and calls.

All three directions below run on the same locked palette — white ground, near-black text, one blue — and the same Romanian copy taken from the live site. What differs is where the budget goes: A spends it on typography, B on photography, C on the shortest path to contact. Pick the trade-off you can live with; the palette, the copy and the mark stay the same in all three.

  • Palette
    Locked by the client: white ground, near-black ink, Apple system blue as the only accent. No second hue in any direction.
  • Copy
    Romanian, lifted verbatim from topcleaning.md. The CTA labels ("Cere o ofertă") are new — the current site has no call to action at all.
  • Photography
    Unsplash placeholders, listed at the end. Swap for the team's own photos before launch; direction B depends on that happening.
  • No invented proof
    No testimonials, client logos, counts, years-in-business or certifications appear anywhere — none exist in the source. Every number shown is derived from the real service checklists.

One thing to decide. #007AFF measures 4.0:1 on white. That clears WCAG AA for large text, icons, borders and focus rings — but not for 15px button labels or inline links, which need 4.5:1. All three directions therefore keep #007AFF as the identity blue and use #0062CC, the same hue one step deeper, wherever small text sits on or in the blue. It reads as the same colour and it passes.

De ce să alegeți serviciile noastre de curățenie?

Noi suntem specializați în oferirea de servicii profesionale de curățenie, având o echipă dedicată de experți care își fac treaba cu pasiune și depășesc așteptările clienților. Am reușit să câștigăm încrederea clienților prin servicii de curățenie de calitate și atenția noastră la detalii.

  • Angajăm doar personal calificat și dedicat, trecând printr-un proces riguros de selecție, pentru a vă asigura cele mai bune servicii de curățenie.
  • Vă economisim timpul prețios, preluând sarcina curățeniei și oferindu-vă mai mult timp liber pentru activitățile importante.
  • Oferim curățenie de calitate, servicii personalizate și atenție la detalii, fie pentru domiciliu, fie pentru birouri.

My recommendation: C — Desk

Build C. This business closes on the phone. There are no online prices, no booking engine and no checkout — step 2 of their own process is literally "we assess, then we quote." A homepage whose job is to get that call should put the number, WhatsApp, Viber and a three-field form within one screen, and C is the only one of the three that does.

  • It is the most honest about what the client actually has. No prices are invented; the only numbers on the page — 13, 9, 13, 3 — come from the real service checklists, and they answer the question a visitor actually has: what am I paying for?
  • It survives the photography problem. Today there are no photos of Top Cleaning's own work. B fails without them and A needs one great one. C needs one, and it can be an office corridor.
  • It is the cheapest of the three to hit the Lighthouse and i18n targets: one hero image instead of six, a table that reflows rather than a photo grid, and layouts that absorb Russian running longer than Romanian.

Two things to borrow. Take A's typographic discipline into C — the hero at 44px with tight tracking, the hairline rules instead of card borders, the restraint with the blue. And keep B's serif for the wordmark only, so the new site still looks related to the logo on their van. That combination gives you a page that converts and does not look like every other service template.

If the goal is the opposite — brand differentiation over booking volume, a site the owner is proud to show — choose A. It is the strongest piece of design of the three and the one most likely to be remembered. It will just book fewer jobs per hundred visitors.
